The runtime loop

  1. Lidar cloud
  2. pointcloud_to_laserscan
  3. amcl + costmaps
  4. planner (A*)
  5. controller (DWB)
  6. velocity_smoother
  7. cmd_vel_bridge
  8. Go2 walks

↺ The robot moves, the lidar and odometry change, and the loop runs again at about 20 Hz until the goal is reached. send_goal then exits 0, or 1 on failure.

Design choices worth knowing

BIM is ground truth

The planner won't route through unknown space. Live lidar only adds obstacles on top of the BIM walls.

Doors and furniture left out

Their state changes, so the lidar catches closed doors and moved furniture at runtime instead.

Two DDS networks

Robot side: CycloneDDS, domain 0. ROS side: Fast DDS, domain 1. Bridges move data between them.

Initial pose

AMCL starts at (0, 0, 0) in the BIM frame unless set in nav2_params.yaml or with RViz from a laptop. Lining this up with the BIM's coordinates is the trickiest part of setup.

Maps are made off the robot

IfcOpenShell needs Python 3.10+, the Jetson has 3.8 and is slow. Only the .pgm + .yaml get copied over, and a new map needs no rebuild.

Two obstacle avoiders

The Go2 firmware has its own avoidance mode, separate from Nav2's. Nothing here sets it, so decide on purpose whether both should run.
